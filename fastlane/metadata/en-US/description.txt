Pixling is a pixel pet that lives on your phone. Feed it, play with it, keep it clean, and watch it grow through four life stages.

It runs completely offline. No account, no sign-up, no network, no ads, no tracking.

CARE IS THE WHOLE GAME
Your pet's hunger, mood, and hygiene move with real time, so you don't need to keep the app open. Come back later and your pet's state reflects how long you were away.

Coins are settled from how well-fed and happy your pet was while you were gone. Care well and you earn more. Neglect your pet and you earn less. There is no separate punishment rule because none is needed.

Each pet has a daily earning cap, so checking in obsessively doesn't out-earn caring properly.

WATCH THEM GROW
Four life stages, each with its own look and its own appetite. Smaller pets eat less. The sprite for each stage is redrawn rather than scaled, so a young pet has a genuinely different head-to-body ratio instead of just looking further away.

FOUR TIERS OF FOOD
From basic scraps up to dried fish. Better food fills more and some tiers lift your pet's mood too. A free option always exists, so you can never get stuck with a hungry pet and no coins.

TWO BREEDS, EIGHT LOOKS
Cats are balanced. Dogs are clingy: their mood drains faster, but they earn more. Four coat patterns each.

DECORATE THE ROOM
Buy furniture with the coins you earn and drag it wherever you like.

29 ACHIEVEMENTS
Across six groups: companionship, growth, care, food, collection, and economy. Most require actually interacting with your pet, not just letting time pass.

TRULY PRIVATE
This app has no networking code in it at all. Your pet, your coins, and your room layout are stored only on your device. Delete the app and everything goes with it, because there is no copy anywhere else. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.

Notifications are optional, off by default, used only to tell you when your pet needs something, and stay quiet overnight.

Available in 20 languages.
